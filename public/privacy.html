Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This policy explains how ZemaHub (the website at zemahub-orthodox.onrender.com and the ZemaHub Android app) handles your information. ZemaHub is developed by Wubgzer Alemayehu.

What we collect

  • Account details — if you create an account: your name, email address and password. Passwords are stored only as a secure one-way hash; we can never see them.
  • Comments you post, shown publicly with your name.
  • Favorites you save, so they follow you between the website and the app.
  • Sign-in session — a random token kept on your device so you stay signed in.
  • Anonymous counts — how many times each mezmur or film is viewed or shared. These counts are not linked to you.

You can browse, watch and save favorites on your device without an account.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no advertising IDs.
  • No tracking or analytics tools.
  • We never sell or rent your information.

Services we rely on

  • YouTube — videos play through YouTube's official embedded player. When you play a video, YouTube (Google) may collect data under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Render hosts the ZemaHub server, and MongoDB Atlas stores the account data listed above.

How long we keep data

Your account, comments and favorites are kept until you delete your account. Signed-in sessions expire after 30 days.

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account and all of its comments and favorites at any time:

  • In the app: Account tab → Delete my account.
  • On the website: sign in → person icon → Delete my account.

Deletion takes effect immediately and cannot be undone. If you can't sign in, email us from your account's address and we will delete it for you.

Children

ZemaHub is a family-friendly religious media service. Accounts are not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: wubgzeralemayehu18@gmail.com
